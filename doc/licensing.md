# AlifeAmbience source licensing

Every addon AlifeAmbience draws sound from, and the basis for using it: either the author granted permission, or the addon's license permits it. The readme Credits section derives from this record.

## Permission is not mod-specific

The authors who granted permission did so for my S.T.A.L.K.E.R. mods generally, not for one mod. The same grant covers AlifeSpooks and AlifeAmbience alike, and there is nothing to extend per mod.
Freely licensed sources (Public Domain, Creative Commons) need no grant at all.

## Addons used, and the basis for each

- Dark Signal Amplified Soundscape, by Shrike. The config spine and the birds. PERMISSION granted 2026-08-15, Discord (shrike0555). His page asks makers to contact him first, and we did.
  https://www.moddb.com/mods/stalker-anomaly/addons/dark-signal-amplified-soundscape
- Audio Expansion, by AniHVX. Insects and frogs. PERMISSION granted 2026-08-16, Discord (Ani HVX).
  https://www.moddb.com/mods/stalker-anomaly/addons/audio-expansion
- Soundscape Overhaul, by Solarint. The environment core (wind, weather, birds, foliage). LICENSE: Public Domain (moddb license field), credit only, no grant needed.
  https://www.moddb.com/mods/stalker-anomaly/addons/soundscape-overhaul-2
- Immersive Ambience Expansion, by Kutee. Wind reinforcement and the helicopter. LICENSE: Creative Commons (moddb license field, the author's page says "feel free to copy"), credit only, no grant needed.
  https://www.moddb.com/mods/stalker-anomaly/addons/immersive-ambience-expansion

## Considered, not used

Recorded for provenance. Their audio does not enter AlifeAmbience.

- RETUNE Ambience Sounds (Aphrodite_child) and myRETUNE Antares (the AntaresWolverine variant). Dropped: the roster took best-of-breed per category, and measured, Antares is RETUNE plus loudness
  (90% byte-identical audio), 48% stereo, no unique content. Permission for RETUNE was granted 2026-08-16 (Discord The_aphrodite_child) and still stands if ever re-added.
- Shrike's unreleased interior audio (slam, interior winds). Not used in the build, covered by Shrike's grant above if re-added.
- DS Overhaul Atmospherics. Not used (ships no presets).

## Distribution

Every addon above carries a free license or the author's permission. The readme credits each author, and the build includes nothing without a free license or the author's consent.
