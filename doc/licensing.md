# AlifeAmbience source licensing

The license of every sound source AlifeAmbience uses. The mod includes only free content or content whose
author granted permission for my mods. The readme Licensing section is derived from this record.

## Permission is not mod-specific

The authors below granted permission for my S.T.A.L.K.E.R. mods generally, not for one mod. The same grant
covers AlifeSpooks and AlifeAmbience alike; there is nothing to extend per mod. Free sources (Public Domain,
Creative Commons, GSC-original) need no grant at all.

## Principle

GSC owns the original S.T.A.L.K.E.R. game audio, and the scene uses it under GSC's long tolerance of
modding. A pack that re-encodes or remasters GSC material does not own it. Any sound that exists in vanilla
Anomaly or in the standalone builds is GSC-original, sourced from there, and free of a pack author's claim.
A pack author's terms govern only the sound they created from scratch. The moddb License field is a coarse
default; the record below reads each author's own words.
